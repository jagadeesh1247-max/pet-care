Pet Parent Guide

Daily Habits for a Longer, Happier Life

Simple, veterinarian-approved routines you can practice at home to keep tails wagging and whiskers twitching year-round.

Daily Nutrition & Hydration

Fresh Water & Mindful Portions

Measure meals using a standard cup rather than eyeballing to prevent gradual weight gain, and refresh water bowls twice daily.

💡 Tip: Cats prefer wide, shallow stainless or ceramic bowls placed away from food.
Dental Hygiene

The Two-Minute Brush Routine

Over 80% of pets show signs of dental sensitivity by age three. Brushing just three times a week drastically reduces tartar buildup.

💡 Tip: Always use enzymatic pet-safe toothpaste—never human fluoride paste.
Mental Stimulation

Enrichment & Sniff Walks

Twenty minutes of dedicated "sniffari" walking or puzzle-feeder play tires a dog’s brain as effectively as a brisk one-mile jog.

💡 Tip: Rotate 3–4 toys weekly so old favorites feel brand new again.
Seasonal Care

Paw Protection & Coat Checks

Test pavement with the back of your hand for 7 seconds before summer walks, and check ears and paws for burrs or ticks after park visits.

💡 Tip: Apply natural shea paw balm before winter walks or hot dry days.
